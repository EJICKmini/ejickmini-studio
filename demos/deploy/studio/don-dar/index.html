Don-Dar Hotel sits on Üzeyir Hacıbəyov, near Pushkin's Park — a small city hotel for people who come to Baku to work, wander and sleep well. The hotel's current website describes its rooms with lorem-ipsum text and a placeholder contact email, so this proposal starts there: say what the rooms are, show what they cost, and answer the questions guests actually ask.

Everything on this page is a working draft for the hotel's review — the room facts, the sample rates, the neighbourhood notes. On the live site the hotel's own photography would sit in these frames. Nothing is published; the hotel's real site remains at dondarhotel.az.

Practicalities — answers before the first call

Questions guests actually ask

Every answer below is a draft for the hotel to confirm or correct. Where the live site is silent, this page proposes an answer rather than inventing a fact.

What time is check-in?

Draft answer: from early afternoon; the hotel confirms the exact hour. Early arrivals can note it in the enquiry. [Placeholder — confirm with hotel]

Draft answer: stated clearly on every rate — included, optional, or served at the on-site restaurant. [Placeholder — confirm with hotel]
