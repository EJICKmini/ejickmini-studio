The method — in three steps

Not watching — doing

How a Sciencely session works, drafted from the team's own description of its format.

?01 / Question

Start with «почему?»

A real phenomenon opens the session — something odd enough that the children ask the question before the educator does.

⚗02 / Experiment

Kids run it themselves

Every child handles the experiment in an equipped laboratory, with age-rated materials and lab safety equipment.

!03 / Explanation

Name what they saw

Only after observing: the science behind it, in words for their age — plus notes and experiments to take home.

What this redesign fixes

Broken prices, one language

Found on the live site

On sciencely.az, price fields render as broken placeholders — the «ман.» currency marker with no number — and the whole site is Russian-only, so international parents in Baku cannot see what a program costs or even what it is called. This demo is trilingual-ready (RU · EN · AZ) and prices every program visibly; at launch the team fills the real numbers or removes prices until they are ready to state them.
