Day 1

Almaty hotel pickup at a confirmed hour · drive to Kolsay lakes · first hike as far as the group's pace allows · night in a Saty guesthouse. Times fixed in the written program.

Day 2

Morning transfer to Kaindy Lake · sunken-forest viewpoint walk · return drive with a stops-you-choose break · Almaty by evening. Weather re-check before departure.

Included

Transport from Almaty, guesthouse night, meals as listed in the operator's written program, guide. The operator confirms the exact list.

Not incl.

What this redesign fixes

The pipe-valve form, retired

Found on the live site

On azimut-travel.kz's tour pages, the "Get program and cost" inquiry forms are labeled Подбор запорно-регулирующей арматуры — "selection of shut-off and control valves". Leftover fields from an industrial-pipes website template ask every traveller to configure a valve instead of a trip. This demo replaces them with a traveller form; a real deployment would keep the operator's existing WhatsApp channel as the delivery route.
