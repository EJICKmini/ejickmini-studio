What this redesign fixes

The cafe-information gap, closed

Found on the live EN homepage

finecup.az/?lang=en promotes the cafe in words but shows no cafe drink prices, no opening hours and no availability anywhere on the page — while its header still carries a generic e-commerce search block whose “Popular Searches” suggest T-Shirt, Blue and Jacket: template leftovers from a fashion storefront. The Azerbaijani homepage does sell packaged beans with prices, and the site has online-shop cart links — so the shop layer exists; the cafe layer is what's missing in English.

This demo adds that layer — menu with prices, honest stock badges, sample hours, and an order builder that previews a ticket — all labelled illustrative, all from one dated snapshot the owner can replace with live data later.
